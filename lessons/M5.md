# M5 — Train an object detector (2 × 50 minutes)
**Objective:** Distinguish classification from detection and train on annotated boxes.
1. Demonstrate bounding boxes and confidence scores.
2. Label classroom objects with privacy/consent safeguards.
3. Prepare distinct train, validation and test scene splits.
4. Fine-tune a YOLO model; instructor may train ahead of class.
5. Test new images and adjust confidence threshold.
**Assessment:** Explain precision vs recall and identify annotation errors.
