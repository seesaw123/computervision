# M4 — My first neural network (50 minutes)
**Objective:** Explain labeled images and train a CNN on synthetic shapes.
1. (10 min) Discuss image pixels, classes and labels.
2. (10 min) Generate images and create train/validation/test splits.
3. (15 min) Train a CNN and inspect learning curves.
4. (10 min) Try different drawn shapes; discuss mistakes.
5. (5 min) Exit ticket about dataset bias and overfitting.
**Assessment:** Students explain one likely bias and how to reduce it.
